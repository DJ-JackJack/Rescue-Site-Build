Finished goals stay up. They're proof that small pushes add up.

How goals work. Shelters send their latest figures, and the numbers are updated by hand with the date shown on every goal. If a goal hasn't been updated in two weeks, it says an update is coming instead of showing an old number as if it were current. This site never collects money, never estimates, and never rounds up.
