Animal Guardian Alliance

Help kids learn to read animals.

The Matching game on the Animal Guardian Alliance page teaches kids what animals are telling us with their bodies. Each card is a photo with arrows I draw on it, pointing out things like pinned-back ears or a tucked tail. Some of these moments are hard to catch on camera, so I'm asking for your help.
