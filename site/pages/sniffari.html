Rescues on Sniffari

Take a shelter dog out for the day.

A Sniffari is a day out for a shelter dog: a walk, a car ride, a cup of whipped cream, a nap in the grass. It's the best day of their week, and it helps them get adopted. There are two ways to go:

Shelter programsDay outs with my featured dogs

Way one

Shelter day-out programs around Hampton Roads

Run by the shelters themselves. Sign up on their page, and follow their rules.

Way two

Day outs with dogs I'm featuring

If you've applied to adopt or foster one of my featured dogs, you'll be able to ask to join a day out with them. It's a great way to get to know each other.

How it will work

  • Apply to adopt or foster through the dog's rescue first.
  • Then ask to join a day out with that dog.
  • The rescue has the final say, every time.

Rules and safety come first

  • The full rules and safety guidelines go up here before the program opens.
  • You're never filmed or named without saying yes first.

tae's Sniffari tips

A great day out doesn't cost much.

Most of my day outs cost $6 to $10. Here's what makes them work.

Go slowLet them sniff everything. For a shelter dog, sniffing is the whole point.
Pack lightWater, a bowl, poop bags, a few treats, and a towel for the car.
Take photosGood photos of a happy dog help them get adopted. Send them to the shelter.
Follow their rulesEvery shelter sets its own. Read theirs before you go, every time.
