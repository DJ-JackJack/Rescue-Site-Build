About

I'm tae.

I educate humans to live in harmony with animals, through rescue, education, and calling out harm. Small creator, outsized impact: most of my featured dogs are home within 3 months of a video.

How it started

It started with eBay, my horse. Ten years together this Halloween, and the beginning of Willow Woods Equine Sanctuary, which is now my official partner.

It's never just content. It's a system, and it's personal. Everything I build is meant to be low-energy and high-impact: dog day outs that cost a few dollars, education that people actually message me about, and videos where the cute carries something serious.

What I believe

We're big on consent, and on portraying humans and animals accurately, but with dignity and consideration. I never use suffering for clicks.
