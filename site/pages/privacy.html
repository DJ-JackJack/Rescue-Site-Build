Privacy

What this site keeps, and what it doesn't.

The short version: no cookies, no tracking and no ads. If you send a message, it's kept so it can be answered, and it's never sold or shared. If you send fan art, your email is deleted once the art has been looked at.

Messages you send

The forms on this site send your email address, your name if you give it, your message, the topic you picked, any boxes you ticked, any other details you fill in (like a video link or your shelter's name), and the page you sent it from. It's stored in the site's database, and only the people who run the site can read it. It's used to reply to you, and for nothing else.

Messages are kept until they're deleted. A deleted message is kept for 90 more days, in case it was deleted by mistake, and then it's cleared out automatically. To have yours deleted, send a message asking, from the same email address.

When a message arrives, the people who run the site can get a phone notification with its topic and the name you gave. The message itself isn't in the notification.

Fan art you send

The Fan Art page's form sends your art, the credit you'd like, and, if you fill them in, a title, a link to where people can see more of your art, and a note. It also sends your email address, so you can be asked a question about your art. Your browser redraws the art before it's sent, so the location, camera and date details hidden in a photo never leave your device.

Until it's looked at, your art and your details are stored privately, and only the people who run the site can see them. If your art is approved, it goes on the Fan Art page with your credit, and your title and link if you gave them. By ticking the box on the form, you also say it can be shown in {{owner_first}}'s videos and posts, credited the same way. Your email and note are never shown. Whether it's approved or not, the copy you sent, your email and your note are then deleted, straight away. A notification about new art says only the credit you gave.

To have your art taken off the site, send a message asking.

Supporters

The Supporters page only lists people who asked to have their name there, the way they asked. Nothing comes from Patreon on its own. To have your name taken off, send a message, or message {{owner_first}} on Patreon.

Visits

Visits are counted as totals: how many times each page was viewed each day, and roughly where visits came from, like TikTok or a Google search. There are no cookies, nothing is saved on your device, and nothing is kept that could identify you.

If someone tries a page that isn't here, the address they tried is counted too, like /happy-tails, so a broken or mistyped link can be found and fixed. Only the page part is kept, never anything after a ? or # in the link, and nothing about who tried it.

The services behind the site

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its content, photos and messages are stored with Supabase. Like any web service, they keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security and to keep things running, under their own privacy policies.

The site's fonts are served by the site itself, so no font service sees your visit.

Videos from YouTube can play right on the site, on Rescues on Sniffari and in Twin Squawk posts, but nothing from YouTube loads until you tap play. They play from youtube-nocookie.com, YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode, and YouTube's own privacy policy applies once a video is playing.

Links to other sites, like shelters, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Patreon, Amazon and Buy Me a Coffee, take you to places with their own privacy policies.

Kids

This site isn't meant for children to send messages or fan art. If you're under 13, please ask a parent or guardian to get in touch, or to send your art, for you.

When a parent or guardian sends art their child made, the art is credited the way they choose. We ask for a first name or nickname only.

The Animal Guardian Alliance runs on Patreon, where a parent or guardian sets up and manages each child's private profile, under Patreon's own privacy policy. This site doesn't collect anything else about children.
