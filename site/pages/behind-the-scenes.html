In the works

A series of coloring books for kids.

I'm planning a series of educational coloring books, each one about a different kind of animal. Kids color, and learn how to understand and care for that animal along the way.

One animal per bookEach book focuses on one species: what they need, how they talk with their bodies, and how to be kind to them.
Each book gives backEvery book is planned to support a different shelter or program that works with its animal. A bird book helps birds, a dog book helps dogs.
Made for learningReal facts in kid-sized pieces, with activities that build the same kindness the Animal Guardian Alliance teaches.

Which shelters and programs each book supports, and how, will be shared with each book before it comes out.

Where it started

Mom, I Want a Horse, written with Willow Woods Equine Sanctuary, was the first. It's an activity book about the responsibilities of owning a horse, starring the sanctuary's goofy star, Steele.
