What a partnership looks like

Willow Woods Equine Sanctuary

Horse Tales

We have an ongoing feature series, sharing the individual horses who call Willow Woods home. Horse Tales shares the real personalities and histories one on one.

Part of the events

We haven't just boosted events, we've been part of them... Even playing a scare actor role in their haunted house fundraisers! We don't just sit back and make videos, we get involved in the events that help support the animals.

Built into the work

We don't just see our partners as an occasional task, we build our support into all aspects of our work. My growth is their growth.

I listen

Every shelter works differently. I understand that each animal needs its own angle, so we stay flexible and creative. If a rescue needs me to hold off on filming, steer people toward donations instead of visits, or feature a different animal than I planned, that's what we do. Working with animals and shelters means nothing follows a set plan; instead, I work with the reality in the moment. Partnership is a necessity, not a byproduct, and the animals' needs always take priority.

How I work

My side of the partnership

What I maintain, and won't bend on.

  • Consent to film staff and volunteers.
  • Videos are respectful, honest and created with the goal of sharing something positive.
  • Safety is a must.
  • Fear free: we don't make animals perform.
  • No drama: we work with many organizations and seek to foster connection and cooperation.
