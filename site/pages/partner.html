What a partnership looks like

Willow Woods Equine Sanctuary

What we've done together

  • A children's book, "Mom, I Want a Horse". 50% of its proceeds go to the sanctuary.
  • Features and promo boosts for their horses and events, like their haunted house.
  • A horse-by-horse section on this site, plus their hay goal.

I listen

  • So many people asked to meet the horses that Willow Woods asked me to pause volunteer sign-ups for a while.
  • So I did. This site points people to visits and donations until they say otherwise.
