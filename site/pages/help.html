Help keeping your pet

Struggling to keep your pet? You're not alone.

No judgement here. Most people who give up a pet never wanted to. Keeping animals in their homes through rough times is how shelters empty out, and it's some of the most important work I do.

Tell me what's going on

Food, vet bills, behavior, housing, a bird you're not sure how to care for: send a short message and I'll point you toward help. I read every message, though replies can take a little while.

Send a message

In danger right now? If a person or an animal is in danger, call 911, or your city's animal control for an animal. Leaving an abusive home with a pet? The National Domestic Violence Hotline is 1-800-799-7233 (thehotline.org), and the Safe Havens and RedRover listings below help find a safe place for your pet.
