# Editing the site from your phone

The edit page lives at **`https://terrortwinsflock.com/edit/`**. It isn't in the menu, and search engines are told to skip it.

**Tip:** open it on your phone and use "Add to Home Screen". It then opens like an app, and you stay signed in.

## Signing in

Use your email and password. Let your phone save the password, and you'll only type it once.

Only accounts on the editor list can change anything. A new account goes on the list in two steps:

1. Create it in Supabase: Authentication → Users → Add user → Create new user. Tick "Auto Confirm User".
2. Add its email to the editor list.

Forgot your password? It can be reset in Supabase: Authentication → Users → pick the account.

## The start screen

**Needs attention** lists what's due, with one-tap buttons:

- **New messages** from the forms on the site.
- **A dog who's still looking and hasn't been checked in 3 weeks.**
  - Tap **Still available** once you've confirmed they're still at the shelter.
  - Tap **Adopted!** if they've gone home.
  - A dog who isn't checked for 4 weeks hides itself, so the site never shows a dog who's already gone.
- **Goal numbers that are getting old.** After 14 days, the site says "an update is coming" instead of showing old numbers.
- **Day-out programs and help listings** that haven't been checked in a while.

Below that, **Everything** has a button for each part of the site.

## Common jobs

| I want to… | Do this |
|---|---|
| Add a dog I filmed | Animals → **+ Add animal**. Fill in the name, tap **Still looking**, pick the rescue, add a photo and the link to their listing, then tap **Add**. |
| Move an animal to the Animal Story Spotlight | Open them, and under **Which part of the site?** tap **Animal Story Spotlight**, then **Save changes**. Tap **Animals We've Helped** to move them back. The Spotlight is for animals already in a forever home or a sanctuary, like the Willow Woods horses; Animals We've Helped is for animals you featured to find a home. Horses switch by themselves when you pick their status (Still looking keeps them in Animals We've Helped), and you can still change it by hand. |
| Mark a dog adopted | Open them, tap **Adopted** (today's date fills in by itself), then **Save changes**. Or use **Adopted!** on the start screen. |
| Tell an animal's story | Open them and write it in **Their story** (leave a blank line between paragraphs). People see it when they tap the animal on Happy Tails. |
| Follow a dog's journey | Open them and scroll to **Their journey**. Tap **+ Add a step** for each visit or thing that happened ("Second day out", "Went to a foster home", "Adopted"), with a date and as much detail as you like. **Fill in from the dates** gives you a start. The arrows change the order. Left empty, the site makes the steps from the video and adoption dates. |
| Add an update or note | Open the animal, then **Updates and notes** → **+ Add an update**. It starts with today's date. The newest shows first in their pop-up. |
| Add a day-out video | Day-out videos → **+ Add day-out video**. Paste the link (on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube or Facebook: **Share** → **Copy link**), add a title and the date, and a cover photo if you like. It shows on Rescues on Sniffari and opens the video where you posted it. |
| Add a dog-friendly place | Dog-friendly places → **+ Add dog-friendly place**. Add the name, the city or the public place, and why you pick it. It shows on Rescues on Sniffari. |
| Update a shelter goal | Shelter goals → the goal. Change the **So far** numbers and tap **Save changes**. The "updated" date on the site changes by itself. |
| Finish a goal | Open it, tap **Goal met 🎉**, then save. It moves to "Goals met". |
| Add an event | Events → **+ Add event**. Events disappear from the site by themselves the day after. |
| Add a rescue | Rescues & shelters → **+ Add**. Use their full proper name. They only show once **"They've said yes to being featured"** and **"Show on the site"** are both on. |
| Add local help | Local help list → **+ Add**. It shows on the "Help keeping your pet" page under its kind of help. A listing can also have an email, a second link (like a free resource library) and a **Caution**, which shows in a yellow box, like "This is a credit card, not financial assistance." Phone numbers can have extra words ("option 1"); the call button dials the first number. |
| Change any wording | **Page wording**, then pick the page, or type a few of the words in the search box. Change the words and tap **Save changes**. Or, while you're signed in, open the page on the site and tap **✏️ Edit this page's wording** at the bottom. |
| Undo a wording change | Open it in Page wording and tap **Use the original**, or empty the box, then save. |
| Answer a message | Messages → **Reply by email** opens your email app. Then tap **Mark done**. |
| Get a notification for new messages | **Messages** → **Turn on notifications**, then **Allow**. Do it on each phone or computer you want them on, and tap **Send a test** to check. On iPhone or iPad, add the editor to your Home Screen first (Share → Add to Home Screen) and turn them on from that icon. |
| Undo a delete | **Recently deleted** → **Restore**. Anything deleted in the last 90 days comes back just as it was. |
| See how many people visit | **Visitors**. Page views for the last 7 days, 30 days or 12 months, which pages are popular, and where visitors came from. |

## Good to know

- **Changes show on the site straight away.** Refresh the page to see them.
- **Hide, don't delete.** Turning off "Show on the site" hides something and keeps everything. If you do delete something by mistake, it stays in **Recently deleted** for 90 days.
- **Links in your bios:** add `?from=tiktok` (or `?from=instagram`, `?from=youtube`) to the site link, so Visitors can tell where people came from. Visits from inside those apps often show as "Direct" otherwise.
- **Wording:** every piece of wording on the site is in Page wording: headings, paragraphs, buttons, the menu and footer (under **On every page**), page titles and descriptions for Google and link previews, photo descriptions, and the messages the site writes itself, like "Still looking" or "Thanks! Your message reached Taea" (under **Messages and labels**). Words in {curly brackets} are filled in by the site, so keep them. Changes show on the site straight away; link previews and Google catch up overnight.
- **Notifications** show the topic and the sender's name, never the message itself. Tapping one opens Messages. To stop them on a device, open Messages there and tap **Turn off on this device**.
- **Your own visits don't count** while you're signed in here. On the Visitors screen you can also skip a phone or computer when it's signed out.
- **Photos:** pick any photo from your phone. The edit page shrinks it and strips hidden data (like where it was taken) before uploading. Still check the background: no house, street, plates or mail.
- **Private stuff never goes in:** no home addresses, no wildlife rehab or release spots, no names of people who haven't said yes. Public buildings like shelters and vet hospitals are fine.
- **Sniffari videos and places** stay hidden on the page until there's at least one of each to show.
- **Messages** are only visible to signed-in editors.
